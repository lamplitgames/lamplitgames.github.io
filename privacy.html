Privacy Policy

The Returns Desk: A Cozy Library

Last updated: 23 October 2026

This policy covers the game The Returns Desk: A Cozy Library, made by Lamplit, on Meta Quest and on Steam. It says what data the game uses, what it is used for, who holds it, and how to have it deleted.

Processed on your device, not on our servers

On Meta Quest the game processes your Meta user ID, profile and age category on your headset, to run achievements and Meta's age check. Lamplit runs no servers and does not receive or store any of it. The game has no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no account of its own.

The store keeps the rest

Your achievements live with Meta or Steam, and your save lives on your device.

Deletion is one email

Ask us to delete your data. We answer within 30 days.

On Meta Quest: the data the game uses

The game uses Meta's Platform SDK for achievements and for Meta's age check. Through it, the game processes:

Your Meta user ID

What it is
The number that identifies your Meta account.
How it is processed
On your headset, through the Platform SDK, when the game records the achievements you earn against your Meta account, so they stay with you between sessions, and when it asks Meta for your account's age category.
Kept
Not saved by the game, and not sent to Lamplit or to anyone other than Meta.

Your Meta user profile (username and profile picture)

What it is
Your Meta username and profile picture.
How it is processed
Meta's achievements feature makes it available to the game through the Platform SDK while you play. The game processes it only on your headset, and does not show it, save it or send it anywhere.
Kept
Not saved by the game, and not sent to Lamplit or to anyone other than Meta.

Your account's age category

What it is
Child, teen or adult.
How it is processed
The game collects it from Meta through the Platform SDK once each time it starts, because Meta requires this of every game open to players aged 10 and up. It is processed on your headset to complete that check, and the game changes nothing based on the answer.
Kept
Not saved by the game, and not sent to Lamplit or anyone else.

Your achievements

How it is processed
When you earn one, the game tells Meta, through the Platform SDK, to unlock it for your Meta account.
Kept
Recorded by Meta against your Meta account.

When the game starts, Meta's platform also checks on the headset that your account owns the game. The game only learns yes or no.

On Steam: the data the game uses

Your Steam account

Used for
Recording the achievements you earn against your Steam account.
Kept
By Steam.

Rich presence

What it is
While you play, the game tells Steam what you are doing, for example "Shelving books: 120 of 864, 3 of 8 wings open".
Shown to
Your Steam friends.

Discord status (Windows only)

What it is
If the Discord app is running on your computer, the game tells it what you are doing, for example "Shelving books: 120 of 864 books, 3 of 8 wings open", and how long you have been playing. The game does not log in to Discord and never sees your Discord account.
Shown to
Whoever Discord shows your activity to, under your own Discord activity settings.
Kept
Not by us. The line goes only to the Discord app on your own computer and is cleared when you quit the game.

On both: your save

Your progress and settings are saved as files on your own device. If Meta's Cloud Backup is on (Meta Quest) or Steam Cloud is on (PC), that service keeps a copy, so your save follows you to a reinstall or a new device.

Who holds it, and for how long

The game processes the data above on your own device while you play. None of it is sent to Lamplit, and Lamplit holds none of it on any server. Meta holds your user ID, profile, achievements and cloud backup under the Meta Platforms Technologies Privacy Policy. Valve holds your Steam account, achievements, rich presence and Steam Cloud copy under the Steam Privacy Policy. If you run the Discord app, Discord holds the status line the game sends it, under the Discord Privacy Policy. Your save stays on your device until you delete it.

We do not sell or share any data. From the stores we receive only aggregate sales reports, which name no player.

If you email us, we receive your email address and your message. We use them only to answer you, and we delete them when you ask.

Children

The game is suitable for players aged 10 and up. Lamplit receives nothing from anyone, children included. On Meta Quest the game processes the age category Meta provides, on the headset, and changes nothing in the game because of it.

Deleting your data

To ask us to delete your data, email lamplit.games@proton.me with the subject "Delete my data" and say whether you play on Meta Quest or Steam. Within 30 days, at no cost, we delete any emails and messages we hold about you and reply to confirm. This works wherever you live.

The data the game processes on your headset (user ID, profile, age category) is not kept by Lamplit, so there is nothing of it on our side to delete. Data stored by Meta or Valve (achievements, cloud backup) can be deleted through your Meta or Steam account or by contacting them under their privacy policies, and if you email us we will help you do it. If we ever cannot delete something for a legal reason, we will tell you why.

Email a deletion request

To delete it yourself

  • Your save: uninstall the game, then remove its cloud copy on Meta's Cloud Backups page or in Steam.
  • Your achievements, user ID and profile: the game does not keep them. They belong to your Meta or Steam account, so ask Meta or Valve to delete them under the privacy policies linked above, or email us and we will help.
  • Your Discord status: there is nothing for us to delete, because the game clears it when you quit. Anything Discord keeps about you is under its own privacy policy.

Contact

For any privacy question or request, write to lamplit.games@proton.me. If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
