Privacy Policy

The Returns Desk: A Cozy Library

Last updated: 25 September 2026

The Returns Desk does not collect, transmit or store any personal data itself. It contains no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no account system.

Your progress and settings are saved as files on your own device. If you use Steam Cloud, Steam synchronizes those files under Steam's privacy policy.

Achievements you earn are recorded by the store you bought the game from, Steam or Meta, under that store's privacy policy. On Meta Quest the game also asks the Meta platform to confirm that you own it.

Your purchase and playtime are handled by that store too. We receive only the aggregate sales reporting the stores provide.
